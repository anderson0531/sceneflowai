Pillar 1 — Standardize All Section Headers on Borderless Typographic Eyebrows Eliminates 8 header blimps (~550px saved)

Replace bordered rounded-full eyebrow capsules in DirectorOperatingSystemSection, VideoServicesSpotlightSection, MobileCompanionPwaSection, ProductionExamplesSection, PricingCredits, InfrastructureSection, TrustSafeguardSection, and ForFilmmakersSection with the clean borderless eyebrow (text-[11px] sm:text-xs font-semibold uppercase tracking-[0.18em]).

Pillar 2 — Replace Multi-Clause Callout Pills with Clean Checklists & Even Grids Zero multi-line rounded-full wraps
  • Gateway & Mobile Step Cards: Render card badges as clean borderless accent kickers above titles; simplify Mobile Step cards from 3 badges down to [01] + Title + subtle bottom /intake metadata.
  • Video Services, Production Examples & Trust: Convert stacked 75px+105px pills into compact ✓ inline rows, condense languagesBanner (127px) into a crisp 1-line locale strip, and lay out Trust flowSteps in a symmetrical grid-cols-2 sm:grid-cols-4 rounded-xl step grid.
Pillar 3 — Unobstructed 16:9 Media Frames on Mobile (0% Text-Badge Occlusion) 65% → 0% artwork occlusion

Move StudioGuidedTourSection topBadge and bottomBadge outside the 16:9 frame on < sm into a sleek caption bar below the artwork (keeping sm:absolute on larger screens), condense the 3 window-bar buttons into compact single-row controls, and hide the duplicate top-right card.badge inside mobile ProductionStyleCard video frames.

Pillar 4 — Fix 360px Header Overflow, Slim Mobile Cookie Bar & Sync Pre-Launch CTAs 0px overflow · 55% → 14% cookie bar

Switch mobile Header.tsx brand to variant="app" with px-3 sm:px-6 (fixing the 366px overflow), slim CookieConsent.tsx on < md to a compact 2-line bottom bar while suppressing FloatingCTA until dismissed, refine the Hero genre selector, and sync remaining $9 buttons to Request Pre-Launch Access.

Pillar 5 — Thumb-Friendly 36px Video Controls & Tighter Mobile Spacing 36×36px tap targets · -6,000px dead scroll

Increase mobile video control hit-areas in VideoServiceOutputShowcase and FeatureScreenshotFrame from 26×26px to 36×36px (h-8 w-8 sm:h-7 sm:w-7) and tighten mobile vertical section padding to py-14 sm:py-24.
