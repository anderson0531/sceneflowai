## 3) Step-by-Step Studio Production SOP

### Stage 1 — Client Intake & Interview Capture (60 Mins)
1. **Record the Anchor Interview:** Capture the subject in a warm, well-lit living room (smartphone 4K on tripod or HD remote interview) holding a tangible family keepsake (e.g., *Elena Rossi-Chen's 1966 leather recipe notebook*).
2. **Scan Reference Photos:** Collect 3–5 clear photos of the subject in their youth (e.g., age 20–30), key family members, and any historic locations.
3. **Capture Likeness & Voice Consent:** Obtain signed client consent for age-locked visual likeness and multilingual voice cloning.

### Stage 2 — Series Room & Continuity Vault Lock (30 Mins)
1. **Create the Memoir Series Bible:** Paste the interview transcript and chapter outline into **Series Room** to structure 5 emotional chapters.
2. **Lock Age-Bridged Character Sheets:** In the **Reference Library (Continuity Vault)**, create:
   - `CHAR_PRESENT`: Uploaded still from the present-day living-room interview.
   - `CHAR_YOUNG`: Age-locked period character turnaround anchored to the client's vintage photographs (preserving identical eye color, bone structure, and period wardrobe).
   - `PROP_KEEPSAKE`: Reference lock for the family heirloom so the exact object transitions seamlessly from the living-room interview into the historical reenactment.

### Stage 3 — Blueprint & Client Pre-Vis Sign-Off (30 Mins)
1. **Run Audience Resonance™:** Verify emotional pacing, hook strength, and chapter transitions in **Blueprint Room** and **Production Stage**.
2. **Generate Voiced Pre-Vis Animatic:** Run the **Autonomous Production Agent** in Pre-Vis mode (Beat Frames + interview voice track + period score) for ~$2–$4 in credits.
3. **Client Approval Gate:** Share the private `/s/` **Screening Room** link in **Pre-Vis** mode so the family approves all names, dates, wardrobe details, and historical settings *before* final motion video rendering.

### Stage 4 — Hybrid Live Upload + Period Motion Rendering (60–90 Mins)
1. **Upload Live Interview Clips:** Place the recorded on-camera living-room clips onto the opening and closing beats of each chapter.
2. **Render Period Reenactments:** Use **Direct Control** or the **Autonomous Production Agent** (Veo 3.1 + `EXT` continuous beats + Historical Style Guardrails such as 1966 Kodachrome grain) to generate the unfilmed historical scenes.
3. **Match-Cut Polish:** Use **FlowDirector** and **Mixer** to blend the storyteller's spoken voiceover across the transition from the modern living room into the historical reenactment.

### Stage 5 — Multi-Generational Localization & Final Cut Delivery (30 Mins)
1. **Generate Multilingual Family Streams:** Clone the storyteller's vocal timbre to produce localized editions (e.g., Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Mandarin, Arabic, Thai, or English) so grandchildren and international relatives can watch in their fluent language.
2. **Assemble & Deliver:** Stitch the master in **Final Cut** and deliver the family's private **Screening Room** premiere link plus downloadable 4K MP4 archives.

## 4) Quality Assurance & Compliance Checklist

- **Gate A — Historical & Cultural Authenticity:** Verify zero modern anachronisms (smartphones, modern cars, LED lights) in period reenactment beats using SceneFlow's Historical Style Guardrails.
- **Gate B — Likeness & Heirloom Continuity:** Confirm `CHAR_YOUNG` facial geometry and `PROP_KEEPSAKE` appearance remain locked across all chapters.
- **Gate C — Audio & Lip-Sync Fidelity:** Ensure smooth -12 dB music ducking under spoken interview audio and natural lip-sync on localized family streams.
- **Gate D — Written Likeness Consent:** Store signed family release and voice-cloning authorization prior to final export.
