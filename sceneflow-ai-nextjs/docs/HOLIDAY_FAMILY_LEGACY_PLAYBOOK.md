## 3. The 15-Minute Client Intake & Story Interview Script

*Record this call on your phone/Zoom (with verbal consent at the start) — the transcript feeds directly into SceneFlow's Blueprint & Storyboard generator on Dec 4!*

### Opening (1 Minute)
> "Thanks so much for doing this, [Name]! I'm recording our audio just so I capture every name, year, and detail accurately for [Honoree's Name]'s film. Is it okay if I record?" *(Wait for clear "Yes.")*
> "Don't worry about telling it perfectly—just talk like you're telling a friend around the dinner table."

### The 8 Story Questions (12 Minutes)
1. **Identity & Opening Hook:** *"What is [Honoree's Name]'s full name, birth year, and the 2 or 3 words everyone in the family uses to describe them?"*
2. **Childhood & Era (Age 5–18):** *"Where did they grow up, what decade was that, and what's one vivid childhood story or quirky habit everyone still laughs about?"*
3. **Turning Point / Young Adulthood (Age 18–30):** *"How did they start their career, serve, or meet [Spouse/Partner]? Give me the setting—what year was it, what were they wearing, or where did they meet?"*
4. **Hardship & Resilience:** *"Every great life has a chapter where things got tough. What challenge did they overcome that shows who they really are?"*
5. **Family Traditions & Home:** *"When the family thinks of home with them—a kitchen smell, a favorite chair, a porch, a holiday tradition, a car, or a hobby—what picture pops into your head immediately?"*
6. **Defining Moment of Pride or Love:** *"What is one specific moment where they showed up for someone in the family that nobody will ever forget?"*
7. **Their Voice & Sayings:** *"Is there a phrase, piece of advice, or joke they ALWAYS say?"*
8. **Closing Dedication & Tone:** *"At the end of the film, what exact message or dedication should appear on screen—and do you want the music tone to feel warm/nostalgic acoustic, uplifting cinematic strings, or gentle piano?"*

### Photo Checklist & Logistics (2 Minutes)
Ask the client to send **15–20 photos** labeled roughly by decade (e.g., `1958_childhood.jpg`, `1974_wedding.jpg`, `2024_grandkids.jpg`):
- `3–4` clear close-up face photos across ages (young adult, middle age, current) — *anchors SceneFlow Continuity Vault multi-age likeness!*
- `4–6` milestone photos (wedding, first home, career/service, kids growing up)
- `4–6` warm recent family / grandkids / holiday photos
- **Delivery confirmation:** Confirm pronunciation of every family name and spelling for closing credits.
