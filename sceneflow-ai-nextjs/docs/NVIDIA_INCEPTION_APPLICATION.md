## 2. Executive Summary (250-Word Application Pitch)

**SceneFlow AI** (`https://sceneflowai.studio`) is a multi-tenant generative AI video SaaS studio (`Series Room → Blueprint Room → Production Stage → Final Cut → Screening Room`) engineered to solve the hardest problem in generative media: **multi-episode, long-form narrative and commercial production without character drift, set instability, or prompt fatigue.**

While first-generation AI video tools remain trapped in isolated 5–10 second clips or static corporate talking heads, SceneFlow AI orchestrates a multi-modal deep learning pipeline that takes creators, filmmakers, podcasters, L&D teams, and independent story producers from a plain-language concept or uploaded screenplay (`.fdx`, `.fountain`, PDF) to a 15-to-45-minute publish-ready master across **7 native UI languages** (`EN`, `ES`, `PT`, `HI`, `ZH`, `AR`, `TH`) and **70+ delivery locales**.

Our software introduces four core technical innovations:
1. **Continuity Vault & Multi-Reference Conditioning:** Locks multi-age character turnarounds, wardrobe states, era-specific textures, and hero props across 100+ shots.
2. **Clip-First Bookend Keyframe Director (`t=0s` → `t=T`):** Synthesizes physically consistent start and end keyframes directly from compiled motion prompts to anchor Frame-to-Video (F2V) and continuous dialogue extensions (`EXT`).
3. **Audience Resonance™ (AR) Pre-Render Evaluation:** Evaluates narrative pacing, structural tension, and retention beat-by-beat *before* GPU video inference is expended—cutting wasted video compute by 70%+.
4. **FlowDirector Multimodal Scene Video QA:** Audits rendered scenes against script compliance and visual continuity with 1-click surgical clip pickups.
