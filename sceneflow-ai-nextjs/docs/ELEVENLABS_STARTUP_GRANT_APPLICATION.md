## 2. Copy-Paste Application Pitch: What Are You Building & How Does It Use ElevenLabs?

### Short Summary (100 Words)
**SceneFlow AI** (`https://sceneflowai.studio`) is a multi-tenant generative filmmaking and video SaaS platform for adult creators, podcasters, L&D teams, and independent story producers, taking users from concept to a 15-to-45-minute multi-character master across **7 native UI languages** (`EN`, `ES`, `PT`, `HI`, `ZH`, `AR`, `TH`) and **70+ dubbing locales**. ElevenLabs powers our core audio-first architecture—including our **Voiced Pre-Vis Animatic Firewall** (letting creators approve multi-character dialogue, narration, and pacing *before* spending GPU video compute), **Continuity Vault Voice Identity**, and **Stem-Separated Multilingual Dubbed Streams** that preserve ambient SFX and musical score.

### Detailed Technical & Product Integration (250 Words)
Most generative video tools treat audio as an afterthought—forcing creators to burn hundreds of dollars on silent or mismatched 8-second video clips before discovering that dialogue cadence, emotional delivery, or story pacing fails.

**SceneFlow AI is architected audio-first around ElevenLabs across four core software layers:**

1. **The Voiced Pre-Vis Animatic Firewall (`Production Stage`):** Before a subscriber spends credits on motion video rendering, SceneFlow synthesizes complete multi-character dialogue and warm documentary narration via ElevenLabs TTS, automatically ducking musical underscore (`-12 dB`) and anchoring visual beat durations to spoken timing (`anchoredTiming.ts`). Approving vocal performance in the Animatic first cuts wasted video regenerations by over 70%.
2. **Continuity Vault Voice Identity (`Series Room` & `Continuity Vault`):** Paid SaaS subscribers lock persistent ElevenLabs voice profiles and consented custom voice clones (`1` to `25` slots per workspace) alongside visual character turnarounds—ensuring multi-episode dramas, podcasts, training modules, and adult family biographies maintain identical vocal timbre across chapters and languages.
3. **70+ Language In-Scene Localization (`Screening Room`):** Our multi-track mixer separates ElevenLabs localized dialogue tracks from ambient SFX and musical score so a single master project exports native-sounding localized masters for global YouTube channels and multinational corporate L&D teams.
4. **Narrated Blueprint & Client Review Portals:** Stakeholders reviewing shared `/blueprint/share` portals can listen to ElevenLabs-narrated treatments on mobile in 7 languages with zero login required.

**Compliance & Attribution:** SceneFlow AI is a commercial SaaS product built strictly for adult creators, filmmakers, and business teams (18+)—we do not build apps or content targeting children under 18.
