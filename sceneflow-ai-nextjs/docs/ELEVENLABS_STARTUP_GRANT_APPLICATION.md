## 2. Copy-Paste Application Pitch: What Are You Building & How Does It Use ElevenLabs?

### Short Summary (100 Words)
**SceneFlow AI** (`https://sceneflowai.studio`) is a multi-tenant generative filmmaking and video SaaS platform that takes creators, podcasters, L&D teams, and white-label video operators from a plain-language story to a 15-to-45-minute multi-character master across **7 native UI languages** (`EN`, `ES`, `PT`, `HI`, `ZH`, `AR`, `TH`) and **70+ dubbing locales**. ElevenLabs powers our core audio-first architecture—including our **Voiced Pre-Vis Animatic Firewall** (letting users hear full multi-character dialogue, narration, and pacing *before* spending GPU video compute), **Recurring Continuity Vault Voice Clones**, and **In-Scene Multilingual Dubbed Streams** that preserve ambient SFX and musical score.

### Detailed Technical & Product Integration (250 Words)
Most generative video platforms treat audio as an afterthought—forcing creators to burn hundreds of dollars on silent or hallucinated 8-second video clips before discovering that dialogue timing, emotional delivery, or narrative pacing fails.

**SceneFlow AI is architected audio-first around ElevenLabs across four production layers:**

1. **The Voiced Pre-Vis Animatic Firewall (`Production Stage`):** Before a user spends credits on motion video rendering, SceneFlow synthesizes complete multi-character dialogue and warm documentary narration via ElevenLabs TTS, automatically ducking musical underscore (`-12 dB`) and aligning beat durations to real spoken cadence (`anchoredTiming.ts`). Creators and their clients approve emotional performance in the Animatic first—cutting wasted video regenerations by over 70%.
2. **Continuity Vault Voice Identity (`Series Room` & `Video Memoirs`):** Subscribers on Starter (`$49/mo`), Pro (`$149/mo`), and Studio (`$599/mo`) lock persistent ElevenLabs voice profiles and consented custom voice clones (`1` to `25` slots per workspace) alongside visual character sheets—ensuring Episode 10 of an animated series or a 5-chapter family biography maintains identical vocal timbre across chapters and languages.
3. **70+ Language In-Scene Localization (`Screening Room`):** Our multi-track mixer separates ElevenLabs localized voice tracks from ambient SFX and musical score so a single master project exports native-sounding streams for global YouTube channels, multinational workforce training SOPs, and cross-border property tours.
4. **Narrated Blueprint & Audience Resonance Reviews:** Clients reviewing shared `/blueprint/share` portals can listen to neural-narrated story treatments on mobile in 7 languages with zero login required.
