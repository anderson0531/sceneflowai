# HERO VIDEO — "From the First Spark"

Landing-page hero video script for **https://sceneflowai.studio**. This is the
hero production script. The previous "Build Worlds. Not Just Clips." script is
retired.

The picture is what a muted autoplay loop can carry. The opening line is what
SceneFlow can do that a traditional dub cannot: the same shot, spoken natively,
in seven languages.

- **Purpose:** Introduce SceneFlow Studio as one guided pipeline — from the first
  idea to a finished master — with continuity, an animatic before the expensive
  render, and native-language performances.
- **Runtime:** 1:20. Eight blocks, **10 seconds** each.
- **Master:** Gemini Omni Flash image-to-video. Every generation is **10 seconds**
  (Omni maximum). Upscale to **4K** in post.
- **Delivery:** 16:9, 24 fps, cinematic live action. Real rooms, real camera
  moves, physically plausible motion.
- **Languages:** English, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Chinese, Arabic, Thai.
  Block 01 is generated **seven times** (native mouth shapes). Blocks 02–08 are
  generated **once**, with nobody speaking on camera, and a voiceover in all
  seven languages.

Follows the block convention of
[`cosmic-roommates-animated-comedy.md`](./cosmic-roommates-animated-comedy.md)
and [`1847-maple-drive-listing-tour.md`](./1847-maple-drive-listing-tour.md).

---

## OBJECTIVE

Land the current landing promise, in this order, without quoting the page:

1. **From the first spark to the finished master.** A film, not a loose clip.
2. **One pipeline.** Story, audience, and look are locked in Blueprint before
   frames exist.
3. **Continuity.** Cast, voices, wardrobe, and places stay the same person and
   the same coat from scene to scene. The Reference Library is the mechanism;
   the picture shows the result.
4. **Direct, then render.** Approve the film on the animatic, then pay for the
   finished picture.
5. **Every audience.** The opening is already in the viewer's language. The
   voiceover adds the rest: one master, 70+ languages, every kind of story.

Payoff: the finished rooftop frame, then a post-designed wordmark and
`sceneflowai.studio`. The page button stays beside the player. Do not generate
a call-to-action inside the picture.

Claims that do not belong in this film: "Build Worlds. Not Just Clips.",
"SceneFlow AI Studio", a 40-hour-to-25-minute claim, or a 5× generation claim.

---

## HOW TO GENERATE EACH BLOCK

Two prompts per clip. Both are paste-ready. Append nothing that is not already
inside the prompt.

1. **Still plate.** One photographic frame. Lock identity, wardrobe, set, and
   object counts here. Generate it, approve it, and do not continue until the
   still passes the regenerate rules at the bottom of this document.
2. **Image-to-video, 10 seconds.** Use the approved still as the first frame.
   The video prompt describes only the motion delta. Duration is 10 seconds,
   not 8.

Blocks 05, 06, and 07 need more than one generation to cover a cut inside a
10-second window. Each of those generations is still a full 10-second Omni clip.
Assembly trims them. Do not ask Omni for a 3-second or 5-second file.

**Block 01 is the only sync performance.** Her mouth is closed in the still.
Lips move only while the line is spoken, then close again. Generate her seven
times from the **same still** and the **same camera**. Each video prompt
contains **only that language's line**. Do not dub English mouth shapes.

Blocks 02–08: mouths in frame stay closed. Voiceover is recorded afterward.

## SPOKEN LINE — BLOCK 01 ONLY

She looks into the lens and says one line. Calm, direct, not a shout. About
three to four seconds of speech inside the ten-second clip, leaving a turn
before it and a closed-mouth hold after it.

| Locale | Line | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| English `en` | It starts with a spark. This is your film. | 9 words. Locked. |
| Spanish `es` | Empieza con una chispa. Esta es tu película. | Slightly longer. Speech may run to 0:08. |
| Portuguese `pt` | Começa com uma faísca. Este é o seu filme. | Brazilian Portuguese. |
| Hindi `hi` | शुरुआत एक चिंगारी है। यह आपकी फ़िल्म है। | If it overruns 0:08, do not speed her mouth. Trim the hold. |
| Chinese `zh` | 从一点火花开始。这是你的电影。 | Shorter. Let the closed-mouth hold breathe. |
| Arabic `ar` | البداية شرارة. هذا فيلمك. | Right-to-left caption in post. Picture direction does not flip. |
| Thai `th` | เริ่มจากประกายไฟ นี่คือภาพยนตร์ของคุณ | |

These six translations carry the same meaning and a similar breath. A native
speaker signs off on each line before generation. Do not add a second sentence
to "explain" the product. The seven mouth shapes are the demonstration.

**Timing inside every Block 01 generation**

| Time | Picture |
|------|---------|
| 0:00–0:02 | Still pose. Match already lit. Mouth closed. Eyes just off lens. |
| 0:02–0:03 | She turns her head to the lens. Mouth still closed. |

If a language needs the extra second, speech may finish by 0:08. It may not
run into the cut at 0:10.
