([`directControlCopy.ts`](../../src/config/landing/directControlCopy.ts)). It
plays after [The Friction We Remove](./hero-video-the-friction-we-remove.md).

- **Promise:** Direct the story. Approve it before you spend on motion.
- **Runtime:** 0:40. Four blocks, **10 seconds** each.
- **Master:** Gemini Omni Flash, still then 10-second image-to-video, 16:9,
  24 fps, upscaled to 4K in post.
- **Who:** Ana, locked to
  [`references/char-ana-reference.jpg`](./references/char-ana-reference.jpg).
  Mouth closed. Her voice is the voiceover in English, Spanish, Portuguese,
  Hindi, Chinese, Arabic, and Thai. One picture cut.
- Lena is the drama subject from the hero. Hair down, navy trench, silver
  locket. She does not speak.

The rooftop ending rhymes with Block 06 of From the First Spark: the same
locked camera, the same work light, the same switch, Ana's left hand.

## OBJECTIVE

1. Ana sets the cast, the wardrobe, and the place. The Reference Library holds
   them.
2. She sets the beat in plain words. No prompt engineering on screen.
3. She watches the animatic and can still change a shot.
4. Only then does the finished light come on.

Do not show agents as robots, floating panels, or a timeline UI. The control
is her hand, the wall, the screening, and the switch.
